# Upgrade notes: Phase 4 (catalog redesign)

## Required actions

1. `composer install` (adds `kalnoy/nestedset` and `intervention/image`).
2. `php artisan migrate`. All changes keep existing data:
   - **Activity log:** catalog models moved to `PnShop\Catalog\Models`, and activity log rows are rewritten to morph aliases (`product`, `category`, …).
   - **Categories:** they become a nested set built from `parent_id`, and `sort_order` is dropped. A `category_product` table holds every product–category link, filled from `product_category_id` (which stays as the primary category).
   - **Brands:** new `brands` table, and products get `brand_id`.
   - **Media:** new `media`, `media_translations` and `mediables` tables.
   - **Variants:** every product becomes a product with one **default variant**. `sku`, `barcode`, `price`, `discount_price` (now `sale_price`) and `stock` move from `products` to `product_variants`; stock moves into `stock_levels` at a new default location, with an opening-balance movement.
   - **Order lines:** they get `product_variant_id` and `variant_label`, and `order_items.discount_price` is renamed `sale_price`.
   - **Attributes:** they get `is_filterable` and `position`.
   - **Related products:** new `product_relations` table.
3. `php artisan storage:link` (images on the `public` disk), and run a queue worker for image conversions.
4. `npm run build`.

## Behaviour changes

- Carts are keyed by variant. Carts in progress during the upgrade are emptied once.
- `POST /cart` accepts `variant_id`; `product_id` still works for simple products. Cart update and remove routes take a variant id.
- A sale price at or above the regular price is no longer applied. Earlier data could contain such prices, and the storefront showed one price while checkout charged the other.
- Products without an active variant are not listed in the store.
- Paths that are never localized (`/admin`, Livewire, debug assets) are also served under a language prefix, for example `/bg/admin`.

## For developers

- `App\Models\Product`, `ProductCategory`, `ProductAttribute`, `ProductAttributeValue` → `PnShop\Catalog\Models\Product`, `Category`, `ProductAttribute`, `ProductAttributeValue`. `App\Traits\HasSlug` → `PnShop\Foundation\Concerns\HasSlug`; `HasSortOrder` is removed.
- `Product::$price`, `$sale_price`, `$sku`, `$barcode`, `$weight` and `$stock` are shortcuts to the default variant (see [ecommerce/catalog-and-inventory.md](../ecommerce/catalog-and-inventory.md)).
- Change stock only through `PnShop\Inventory\InventoryService`.
- CI runs the test suite on MySQL 8.4 and PostgreSQL 17 as well as SQLite.
